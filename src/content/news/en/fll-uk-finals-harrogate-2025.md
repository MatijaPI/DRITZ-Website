On Saturday 3 May 2025, the British town of Harrogate hosted the FIRST LEGO League UK Finals 2025, the country's big national final. Two judges from our society, Društvo DRITZ – one man and one woman – played an important part in this high-profile technology event, organised by IET Education.

Serving on the international judging panel, they helped assess innovative projects and robot performances with real expertise, once again showing the high level of knowledge among our members.

We are proud of our judges, whose volunteer work regularly helps to deliver top-class FLL events abroad, too!
