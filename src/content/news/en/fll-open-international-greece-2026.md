From 8 to 11 May 2026, the FLL Open International Championship Greece 2026 took place near the Greek city of Corinth – and Slovenia shone on every front.

Three volunteers from Društvo DRITZ did an excellent job as part of the judging and refereeing team, while the Slovenian team Eco Robots (OŠ Šentvid primary school) won a trophy against strong international competition.

Our warmest congratulations to our volunteers on their tremendous effort, and to the team on a fantastic, historic achievement!
