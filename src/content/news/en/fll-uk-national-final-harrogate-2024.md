On Saturday 20 April 2024, the English town of Harrogate hosted the UK National Final 2024 – the FIRST LEGO League final for England, Ireland, Wales and Scotland.

At this prestigious event of the MASTERPIECE season, Slovenian expertise was represented by three judges, all members of Društvo DRITZ. As part of the official judging panel, they assessed innovative projects and robot performances, making a significant contribution to the success of the competition.

Congratulations to our trio of judges on a job well done on a major international stage!
