From 18 to 21 May 2023, Morocco hosted the international FIRST LEGO League championship Morocco Open International 2023 – and Društvo DRITZ was there. In the heat of Marrakech, three of our volunteers took part in the SUPERPOWERED season competition, taking their places on the official judging panel.

The tournament ended with a very special celebration: at the closing ceremony, our judge Valentin Šeško received the prestigious trophy for the tournament's top volunteer! The award reflects his exceptional energy, expertise and dedication to the FLL community.

A big thank you and warmest congratulations to the whole trio – and especially to our award-winning top volunteer – on this outstanding international achievement!
