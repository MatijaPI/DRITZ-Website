Sharing knowledge and building connections know no borders – and at Društvo DRITZ we prove it through our ongoing work across the wider region. Every year we take an active part in, and help organise, the national FIRST LEGO League competitions in Croatia, Serbia and Montenegro.

Drawing on their extensive experience and expertise, our members take on key organisational and judging roles at these tournaments. In doing so, we make an important contribution to raising the standard of youth robotics across the Balkans and strengthen our partnerships with FLL organisers in the region.

We are proud to help create unforgettable technology experiences for young minds beyond our own borders, too!
