From 13 to 16 May 2024, the Norwegian town of Bodø hosted the prestigious FLL Open European Championship 2024 – an event that will go down in golden letters in the history of the Slovenian FLL programme and of our society. Against exceptionally strong competition, the top Slovenian team Black&White proudly became European champions!

Two judges who are members of Društvo DRITZ were also part of this success on site, working on the judging team to ensure expert judging of the very highest standard.

Our warmest congratulations to the whole Black&White team on this unique and fantastic European success – and to our two judges!
