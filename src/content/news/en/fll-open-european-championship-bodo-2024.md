From 13 to 16 May 2024, the Norwegian town of Bodø hosted the prestigious FLL Open European Championship 2024 – an event that will go down as a golden chapter in the history of the Slovenian FLL programme and of our society. Against exceptionally strong competition, top Slovenian team Black&White became European champions!

Two Društvo DRITZ members were also on site as part of the judging team, ensuring expert assessment of the very highest standard.

Our warmest congratulations to the whole Black&White team on this fantastic European success – and, of course, to our two judges!
