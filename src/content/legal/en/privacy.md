## Data controller

The controller of your personal data is:

**Društvo za raziskovanje in izobraževanje o tehnologiji in znanosti (Društvo DRITZ)**\
Medlog 52A, 3000 Celje, Slovenia\
Email: [info@dritz.si](mailto:info@dritz.si)\
Phone: [+386 31 251 237](tel:+38631251237)

This policy explains what data we process when you visit our website or get in touch with us, and what rights you have.

## At a glance

- The website does not set any cookies.
- We do not use any tracking, analytics or advertising tools.
- The site contains no third-party content (such as embedded videos, maps or social media buttons). Our fonts are self-hosted, so your browser makes no requests to Google Fonts.
- We only process personal data if you write to us or call us, and only so that we can reply.

## Hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service provided by GitHub, Inc. As with any web server, the host may process technical data when you visit, such as your IP address, browser details (user agent) and the date and time of the request. This data may be recorded in server logs used to keep the service secure and running. We have no access to these logs and do not use them.

You can find out more about how GitHub processes data in the [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Cookies and browser storage

The website does not use cookies.

To make the site more convenient to use, we store just a few functional preferences in your browser's local storage (localStorage):

- your chosen language,
- your chosen colour theme (dark or light),
- whether you have dismissed the language suggestion banner.

These preferences stay on your device and are never sent to anyone – including us. You can delete them at any time in your browser settings (by clearing the site data for dritz.si).

## Contacting us

You can contact us by email, by phone or via the contact form on the website.

- **Email and phone:** we process the information you choose to give us (for example your name, email address or phone number and the content of your message).
- **Contact form:** by default, the form opens your own email program with a pre-filled message. You then send it yourself, and your email provider delivers it to info@dritz.si. Where direct form submission is enabled on the site, the form data (name, email address and message) is passed to an external form-processing service solely so that your message can be delivered to us.

We use the information in your message only to reply to your enquiry and keep it only for as long as is necessary for that purpose. We do not use it for marketing and do not share it with third parties unless required to do so by law.

## Analytics

We do not currently use any web analytics. If we decide to introduce privacy-friendly, cookieless analytics in future, we will update this policy before doing so.

## Legal basis

We process personal data in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

- **replying to your messages** – Article 6(1)(b) GDPR where your enquiry relates to working with the society or entering into an agreement with it; otherwise Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries we receive);
- **technical operation and security of the website** – Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in running the site securely and reliably).

Storing preferences in your browser's local storage is strictly necessary for features you have chosen to use and is not used for tracking.

## Your rights

With regard to your personal data, you have the right to:

- access the data we hold about you,
- have inaccurate data corrected,
- have your data erased,
- restrict processing,
- object to processing based on legitimate interests,
- data portability.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at [info@dritz.si](mailto:info@dritz.si). We will respond without undue delay and in any event within one month.

If you believe that we are processing your personal data unlawfully, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the [Information Commissioner of the Republic of Slovenia](https://www.ip-rs.si/en/) (Informacijski pooblaščenec).

## Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time, for example when the website or the law changes. The current version is always published on this page, and the date of the last update is shown above.
